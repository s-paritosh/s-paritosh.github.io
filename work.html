My role

Adapted standardised Swaraj brand assets to individual dealership conditions across India — coordinating dealer and regional confirmations, preparing production-ready artwork, managing approvals, and tracking dispatch through to installation closure.

Pipeline
Site intake → Artwork & mockup → Dealer confirmation → HO approval → Production → Dispatch → Installation & closure

How each site moves

Site intake
Dealership shares photographs, fascia dimensions, and regional requirements. Reviewed for completeness before artwork begins.
Artwork & fascia mockup
Standard brand assets adapted to the site — dimensions, obstructions, regional language script, and actual site conditions. Output is a production-referenced layout for dealer review.
Dealer confirmation
Mockup shared with dealership and territory team. Revisions incorporated where site conditions or details were updated.
Head Office approval
Artwork submitted to brand Head Office for sign-off. Production not released without duly approved files.
Production & dispatch
Approved files prepared for manufacturing. Production status tracked; dispatch coordinated with logistics.
Installation & closure
Delivery and execution tracked post-dispatch. Site photographs collected after installation. Warranty and closure coordinated where applicable.
