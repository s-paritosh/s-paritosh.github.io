Case Study
Automotive Branding Pan-India · Ongoing
Nationwide Dealership Branding Implementation
Swaraj (Mahindra & Mahindra)  ·  500+ dealership sites
My role

Adapted standardised Swaraj brand assets to individual dealership conditions across India — coordinating dealer and regional confirmations, preparing production-ready artwork, managing approvals, and tracking dispatch through to installation closure. 500+ sites processed through this pipeline to date, with the rollout ongoing.

How each site moves
Site intake → Artwork & mockup → Dealer confirmation → HO approval → Production → Dispatch → Installation & closure
Site intake
Site data — photographs, fascia dimensions, and regional specifications — is received and reviewed for completeness. Discrepancies or missing details are resolved with the respective state or territory team; execution approach is aligned internally before any artwork is initiated.
Artwork & fascia mockup
Brand assets are adapted to site-specific parameters — fascia dimensions, structural constraints, regional language script, and on-ground conditions. Output is a production-referenced fascia layout, prepared to brand specification and submitted for dealer review.
Dealer confirmation
Artwork is circulated to the dealership and territory team for review. Feedback is incorporated and layouts revised where site parameters or requirements have changed, ensuring sign-off before proceeding.
Head Office approval
Dealer-confirmed artwork is submitted to brand Head Office for formal sign-off. Non-standard site conditions or deviations are flagged and guidance sought before proceeding. Production release is contingent on duly approved artwork files.
Production & dispatch
HO-approved artwork is released for production. Dispatch details and logistics information received from the production team are communicated to the dealership and Head Office to facilitate timely delivery.
Installation & closure
Post-installation, revamp site photographs are collected from the dealership and documented for project records. Closure is coordinated with the production team and relevant stakeholders upon confirmed successful installation.
